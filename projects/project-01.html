Modern Chair Study

A furniture-modeling study focused on building a chair form, refining its silhouette, and exploring material and lighting variations in Blender.

DISCIPLINEProduct modeling
SOFTWAREBlender
PROJECT TYPE3D modeling study

The brief

The goal was to create a recognizable chair model with a tall backrest, seat, and four legs, then explore how material and lighting choices affect the final presentation.

Modeling & materials

The uploaded process images show the chair at different stages, including wireframe and x-ray views alongside solid viewport views. These help document the model's structure as well as its overall form.

Lighting & final render

The final views explore a pink material against a neutral studio environment. Additional angles show the backrest, seat thickness, legs, and the chair's overall silhouette.

SERVICES / WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

Ideas into form.
Form into visuals.

I’m OverallSpace, an emerging 3D product artist based in Nigeria. I use Blender to practice and develop product models, hard-surface forms, and product renders. For each brief, we can agree on the scope, references, deliverables, and timeline before work begins.

SERVICE / 01

3D Product Modeling

Modeling of product concepts and everyday objects with attention to proportions, silhouette, edges, and visible design details.

SERVICE / 02

Product Rendering

Studio-style presentation renders with considered camera angles, lighting, materials, and composition to communicate a product's form.

SERVICE / 03

Hard-Surface Modeling

Practice and modeling work for rigid objects such as consumer electronics, accessories, and designed forms with defined surfaces and edges.
